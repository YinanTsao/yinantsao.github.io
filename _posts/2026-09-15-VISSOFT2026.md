<h3>Maxime presented at VISSOFT 2026</h3>

On September 15th, 2026, Maxime presented his works at <a href="https://vissoft.io/2026/program.html" target="_blank">VISSOFT 2026</a>.

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: space-around;">
    <img src="{{ site.baseurl }}/images/VISSOFT2026-4.jpg" width="500px"/>

The paper <a href="https://researchportal.unamur.be/fr/publications/visualizing-the-evolution-of-data-access-in-microservices/" target="_blank">Visualizing the Evolution of Data Access in Microservices</a> present new visualizations and metrics to analyze and understand the history of data access in distributed and heterogeneous codebases. These interactive instruments are actionable on our oneline tool called <a href="https://denim.unamurcs.be/" target="_blank">DENIM</a>.
