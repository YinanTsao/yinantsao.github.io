<h3>Welcome aboard, Dr. Bochra Boughzala and Dr. Xiaokun Xu!</h3>

<p>We are delighted to welcome two new postdoctoral researchers, Dr. Bochra Boughzala and Dr. Xiaokun Xu, to the RAINDROP research group at UCLouvain.</p>

<p>Dr. Bochra Boughzala recently earned her Ph.D. from the University of Groningen, Netherlands, where her research focused on accelerating real-time data analytics with in-network computing.</p>

<p>Dr. Xiaokun Xu completed his Ph.D. last year at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, USA, specializing in measuring and improving the Quality of Experience in cloud-based game streaming.</p>
